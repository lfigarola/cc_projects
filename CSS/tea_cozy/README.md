# Tea Cozy Webpage

[This project](https://lfigarola.github.io/cc_projects/CSS/tea_cozy/) is part of a Codecademy exercise, where the goal was to fix and improve an existing webpage based on a provided design mockup. The webpage showcases the fictional tea shop "Tea Cozy," including its mission, featured products and store locations. 

The exercise involved making the webpage match the provided design by correcting the layout, styling, and ensuring the overall design aligned with the [given mockup](./img-tea-cozy-redline.jpg).

## Project Characteristics

- **Fixing an Existing Webpage**: This project is based on fixing a pre-existing, incomplete webpage by improving its layout and styling, ensuring that it aligns with a provided design mockup.
- **Responsive Design**: The webpage was made fully responsive, ensuring it looks good on a variety of devices from mobile phones to desktop screens.
- **Navigation Bar**: A fixed navigation bar was added for easy access to different sections of the page, including "Mission," "Featured Tea," and "Locations."
- **Mission Section**: The mission statement of the tea shop is clearly presented along with a background image to visually reinforce the brand message.
- **Tea of the Month**: The "Products" section displays a grid of featured products with their images and titles, properly formatted according to the design.
- **Locations Section**: This section lists the tea shop’s locations with their respective addresses, presented over a background image.
- **Footer**: Contains business contact information, including the email and phone number.

## Technologies Used

- **HTML**: Provides the structure for the webpage.
- **CSS**: Styles the webpage, handling layout, colors, fonts, and responsiveness.

## Design

The project is based on the design [mockup](./img-tea-cozy-redline.jpg), which was used as a reference to recreate and fix the existing webpage. The webpage layout, colors, and structure were carefully aligned to match the design provided.
